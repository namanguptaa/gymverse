# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want GymVerse to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone: gym owners, trainers, developers and first-time contributors, whatever their background, experience, body, gender, religion, caste, nationality or language.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to technology.
- Give feedback on ideas and code, not on people.
- Accept honest feedback gracefully, and say thank you.
- Respect members' privacy: never post real member names, phone numbers or photos in issues, pull requests or discussions.

## What's not okay

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention
- Discriminatory jokes or comments
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour goes against this code.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (@namanguptaa). Reports are kept confidential.

| Situation | What maintainers may do |
| --- | --- |
| Minor, first time | A private note explaining what was wrong |
| Repeated or more serious | A formal warning and a break from the project |
| Harassment or threats | A permanent ban |
